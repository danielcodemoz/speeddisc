SCHOOL / MANUAL GUIDE

Get the PCs ready before class.

A practical checklist that tests what students will actually use.

What is happening

The lesson starts, but accounts cannot sign in, apps are missing or student computers cannot open the materials.

01

Diagnose

  1. Define the activity and exact apps, sites and files needed. Confirm permissions and the school's data policy with the responsible person.
  2. Test a representative PC with a demonstration student account, not just an administrator account. Open the activity and confirm files can be saved in the approved location.
  3. Check free space, keyboard/mouse, audio if needed, network, projector and printing if part of the lesson. Record the PC number and issue without storing credentials in the report.
02

Fix manually

  1. Prepare and test copies of materials in an approved location. Do not use real student documents as examples or delete other users' work.
  2. With IT, schedule updates and installations outside lesson time. Let restarts finish and test again. Do not disable protections, policies or accounts to work around a block.
  3. For a specific fault, use the disk, network, startup or printer guide. Prepare an offline alternative when the activity allows it and confirm it opens before class.
03

Check the result

  1. Sign in again as the demonstration student: open the app, perform a short task, save, close and reopen the file. Test the links and resources required for the lesson.
  2. Mark ready PCs and exclude those still failing from the activity. Record the date, tests and responsible person; sign out of the demo account.

What changes and how to undo it

The manual check installs and cleans nothing. Installations, updates and data removal are separate interventions approved by the owner, with backups where needed.

Optional tools

Optional for technicians: SpeedDisc-Escola v0.4.0 runs the quick package in SpeedDisc.exe. It removes temporary files/caches and changes the power plan; it is not a classroom-readiness test. It requires the executable in the same folder and administrator rights. Do not run it just to consult this checklist.

When to stop and ask for help

Do not perform bulk maintenance before testing one PC and checking backups. If school policy blocks an action, ask for help rather than bypassing it.
